🚀 Getting Started

Login to Your Account

1 Go to the login page
2 Enter your Student ID (provided by your institution)
3 Enter your Password (provided by your administrator)
4 Click "Sign in as student"
5 You'll be taken to your personalized dashboard
🔐 Security Reminder: Keep your Student ID and password confidential. Never share your login credentials with anyone. Change your password regularly for better security.

📊 Your Dashboard

Your dashboard is your personal command center where you can see your performance at a glance.

Dashboard Overview

Your dashboard displays four key performance indicators:

Metric Description Why It Matters
Average Marks Your average score across all completed tests Shows your overall performance level
Tests Attempted Total number of tests you have submitted Tracks your practice volume and consistency
Current Rank Your position in the global leaderboard Shows where you stand compared to peers

Dashboard Sections

📝 Taking Tests

Tests are the core of your learning experience on Exam Hub. Here's how to access and attempt them.

Accessing Available Tests

1 Click on "Tests" in the main navigation menu
2 You'll see a list of all available tests
3 Each test card shows the title, subject, number of questions, and deadline
4 Check the test status: "Pending" = not yet attempted, "Attempted" = already submitted

Test Information

For each test, you'll see:

  • Test Title: Name of the exam
  • Subject: Category or topic area
  • Questions: Total number of questions in the test
  • Deadline: Last date and time to submit your answers
  • Created By: Your instructor's name

Understanding Question Types

Question Type Format How to Answer
Text Questions Written questions with text and/or images Read carefully and prepare your answer
Image Questions Questions presented as images or diagrams View the image and understand the context
⏰ Time Management Tip: Note the deadline for each test. Don't wait until the last moment to submit. We recommend attempting tests at least a day before the deadline to avoid last-minute technical issues.

✏️ Submitting Your Answers

Once you've answered the questions, you need to submit your responses to the platform.

How to Submit Answers

1 Click on a test to open it
2 Review all questions carefully
3 Prepare your answers (write on paper, take photos, or scan documents)
4 Click the "Upload Answer Files" button
5 Select image files of your answer sheets
6 You can upload multiple files for different answer sheets
7 Click "Submit" to finalize your submission

Submission Requirements

  • File Format: Submit images (JPG, PNG format recommended)
  • Image Quality: Ensure images are clear and legible
  • Content Clarity: Your handwriting must be readable by evaluators
  • Complete Answers: Include answers to all questions you attempt
  • Deadline Compliance: Submit before the test deadline
📸 Submission Tips:
  • Take photos in good lighting conditions
  • Ensure the entire answer sheet is visible in the frame
  • Avoid shadows and reflections in photos
  • Upload files in the correct order (Question 1, 2, 3, etc.)

After Submission

  • You'll receive a confirmation message
  • Your submission status will change to "Evaluated" once the instructor grades it
  • Check your "My Submissions" section to view feedback
  • Your marks will be added to your dashboard automatically
  • You'll see the instructor's comments on your answers

💡 Study Tips & Best Practices

Preparation Strategy

  • Start Early: Don't wait until the last day to attempt tests
  • Read Carefully: Take time to understand questions before answering
  • Organize Answers: Write clearly and number your responses properly
  • Time Management: Allocate time appropriately for each question
  • Review Before Submitting: Double-check your work before final submission

Effective Learning

  • Regular Practice: Attempt all available tests regularly, not just before exams
  • Learn from Mistakes: Review feedback carefully to understand errors
  • Track Progress: Monitor your dashboard to see improvement
  • Set Goals: Target specific rank or average marks to work towards
  • Study Consistency: Dedicate regular time for exam preparation

Technical Best Practices

  • Stable Internet: Ensure good internet connectivity before attempting tests
  • Clear Photos: Take high-quality photos of answer sheets
  • Proper Lighting: Use good lighting when photographing answers
  • File Organization: Keep answer files in logical order
  • Backup Submissions: Submit early to avoid last-minute technical issues

Performance Optimization

  • Understand the marking scheme for each test
  • Focus on high-value questions (higher marks)
  • Analyze the leaderboard to see top performers' averages
  • Study previous feedback patterns to improve faster
  • Build a study schedule based on your available time
🎓 Success Formula: Consistency + Quality Practice + Feedback Analysis + Continuous Improvement = Success

❓ Common Questions & Answers

Can I attempt a test multiple times?

No, each test can typically be submitted only once. Make sure you're ready before submitting your final answers.

What happens if I miss a deadline?

You won't be able to submit answers after the deadline passes. The system closes submissions automatically. Contact your instructor if you have a valid reason for missing the deadline.

How long does it take to get my evaluation?

Evaluation time depends on instructor workload. Typically, expect feedback within 3-7 days. Check your submissions regularly for updates.

Can I change my answer after submitting?

No, once submitted, your answers cannot be changed. The submission is final and locked.

How is my average calculated?

Your average is calculated by dividing the total marks you've earned across all completed tests by the total number of tests attempted.

How often does the leaderboard update?

The leaderboard updates in real-time as evaluations are completed and new scores are added to the system.
